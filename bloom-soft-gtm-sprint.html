Platform Setup Guides — Quick Reference

Etsy

  • Account creation (age 18+ required)
  • Shop setup with "Bloom Soft Co." name
  • Logo upload + branding
  • About section (brand story)
  • Listing workflow (title, tags, description, images)
  • Pricing: $0.20/listing + 6.5% transaction + 3% payment
  • Link-in-bio to social accounts
  • Ads setup ($1/day test budget)

Redbubble

  • Account creation (18+ or parental approval)
  • Upload design files (high res PNG/PDF)
  • Enable all products (posters, hoodies, mugs, etc.)
  • Margin settings (recommend 20–30% markup)
  • Auto-fulfillment (no inventory needed)
  • Link to Etsy for cross-promotion

TikTok

  • Creator/business account setup
  • Bio: "Cottagecore AI art | Etsy shop link"
  • Profile picture (logo)
  • Link-in-bio to Etsy shop
  • First video: "I'm 13 and started..." (viral hook)
  • Post 1–2 times daily (consistency = algorithm)
  • Engage with comments (reply videos, duets)

Instagram

  • Business account (@bloomsoftco)
  • Bio: "AI cottagecore wallpapers + prints | 13-year-old artist"
  • Link to Etsy in bio
  • Aesthetic grid (9 posts minimum for first impression)
  • Stories daily (behind-the-scenes)
  • Reels (10–15 sec design snippets)

Pinterest

  • Business account (NOT personal)
  • Rich Pins enabled (auto-feeds product data from Etsy)
  • Boards: "Wildflower Art", "Cottagecore", "Phone Wallpapers", "Easter"
  • Pin every design to 3–4 relevant boards
  • Use keywords in pin descriptions (cottagecore, AI art, etc.)
  • Pinterest = long-tail traffic (pin today, sales in weeks)
