1 Create Your Etsy Shop

This takes about 10 minutes. You're just setting up the store — no products yet.

Go to etsy.com/sell and click "Get started"
Language: English. Country: United States. Currency: USD.
Shop name: BloomSoftCo (if taken, try BloomSoftShop)
Enter payment info (where Etsy sends your money) and billing (how Etsy charges fees)

That's it for setup. You now have a shop. It's empty, but it's yours.

2 Make Your Shop Look Good

This takes about 5 minutes. Three things to paste in.

Upload shop icon — use the bloom_logo_v4.png file in this folder
Upload shop banner — use wallpaper1_petal_drift.png (Etsy will let you crop it)
Paste this as your Shop Announcement (the short blurb visitors see first):
Shop Announcement
Soft watercolor art for homes and hearts. Digital downloads you can print today. New designs every week. Welcome to Bloom Soft Co.
Go to Shop Manager → Settings → About → paste this as your About section:
About Section
Bloom Soft Co. creates gentle, watercolor-inspired digital art and printables for families who love beauty and meaning. Every design is crafted with soft botanicals, wildflower palettes, and a cottagecore heart. We believe your walls, your journals, and your family moments deserve something beautiful. All products are instant-download PDFs and digital prints — ready to print at home or at your favorite print shop. Based in the US.

This is the big one — but every word is already written for you. For each product below, go to Listings → Add a listing and follow these steps:

For every listing, set these the same:
Type: Digital | Who made it: I did | Finished product | Renewal: Automatic
Category: Paper & Party Supplies → Paper → Journals & Planners
Then paste the Title, Description, and Tags from the boxes below. Upload the PDF file. Upload the cover image from the listing-images/ folder.

4 Turn On Ads

This takes 2 minutes and gets your products in front of people searching for Conference printables.

Go to Shop Manager → Marketing → Etsy Ads
Set daily budget to $5.00
Strategy: "Balance Orders with Returns"
Advertise: All listings

$5/day = $35 for the first week. If it's working, keep going. If not, Dad and Claude will adjust.

5 Tell People
