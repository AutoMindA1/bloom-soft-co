## PRICING INTELLIGENCE

### What Top Sellers Are Charging

**Single Prints (individual files):**
- **Budget tier:** $1.99–$3.99 (entry-level, high volume)
- **Mid-market:** $4.99–$6.99 (most common)
- **Premium:** $7.99–$9.99 (unique, detailed work)

**Bundles (4-10 designs):**
- **Standard:** $12.99–$19.99
- **Premium:** $24.99–$49.99+ (10+ pieces, curated collections)

**Phone/Device Wallpapers:**
- $1.99–$3.99 (impulse purchase; margins lower due to Etsy fees)

**Multi-size Formats (4x6 to 24x36):**
- $4.99–$8.99 (per file or per format)

### Sweet Spot Analysis

**Volume Play ($2.99–$3.99):**
- Higher conversion rate (broader audience)
- Lower AOV per sale
- Requires 20–30 sales/month to hit $100 revenue
- **Volume needed for $2,000/month goal: ~500+ sales**

**Premium Play ($9.99–$14.99):**
- Lower conversion, higher perceived value
- Better margins (6.5% Etsy fee + 3%+$0.25 payment processing)
- Bundles compound this advantage
- **Volume needed for $2,000/month goal: ~150–200 sales** (with mix of $9.99 and $14.99)

**Hybrid Strategy (Recommended):**
- Lead with singles at $4.99–$5.99 (moderate volume, clear value)
- Upsell bundle packs at $14.99–$19.99 (2–3 thematic sets)
- Test phone wallpapers at $1.99–$2.99 (traffic driver, builds reviews)

### Fee Impact on Pricing

On a $5.00 sale:
- Listing fee: $0.16
- Transaction fee (6.5%): $0.33
- Payment processing (3%+$0.25): $0.40
- **Net: ~$3.76/sale (75% margin)**

On a $14.99 bundle:
- Listing fee: $0.16
- Transaction fee: $0.97
- Payment processing: $0.70
- **Net: ~$12.16/sale (81% margin)**

**Offsite Ads Fee (applies once >$10k annual revenue):** 12–15% deduction. Plan for this if scaling aggressively.

### Competitive Recommendation for Bloom Soft Co.

**Month 1 Pricing Strategy:**
- 3–5 singles at **$4.99** (entry point, build reviews)
- 1–2 bundles at **$14.99** (upsell flagship collection)
- 1 budget phone wallpaper set at **$1.99** (viral/traffic)

**Rationale:** Lower risk entry, clear margin testing, and psychological comfort for first-time buyers on a new shop.

## MOCKUP STRATEGIES

### What Best-Performing Listings Show

**Image Count & Types:**
- **Photo 1 (Primary):** Room mockup with framed print on wall (neutral room, soft natural lighting)
- **Photo 2:** Close-up of print (flat lay, shows detail/color accuracy)
- **Photo 3:** Gallery wall mockup (3–5 prints together, styled room setting)
- **Photo 4:** Lifestyle shot (print in context, e.g., on desk, in bedroom, on shelf)
- **Photo 5–7:** Additional format mockups (e.g., phone wallpaper preview, frame size guide)

**Total: 5–7 photos per listing**

### Mockup Style Impact

**Room Scene Mockups (Best for Wall Art):**
- Soft, neutral background (light gray, white, warm beige wall)
- Natural lighting (window light, not harsh shadows)
- Minimalist decor context (avoid overwhelming clutter)
- Gold/wood frame around print (professional frame suggestion)
- **Psychological impact:** Buyers visualize the art in their space

**Flat Lays (Good for Detail):**
- Print on white or neutral background
- Show fine details (watercolor texture, line work)
- Include size reference (ruler, coin, standard objects)
- **Purpose:** Credibility + quality assurance

**Gallery Wall Mockups (Best for Bundles):**
- 3–5 prints arranged together (grid or salon style)
- Styled room background
- Show diversity of color/style within bundle
- **Psychological impact:** Buyers see upsell opportunity

**Lifestyle Shots (Engagement Boost):**
- Print in real context (on desk corner, shelf, bedroom)
- Human elements (plant, coffee cup, hands pointing) optional
- **Purpose:** Emotional connection

### Thumbnail Optimization (Most Critical)

**Etsy crops listings to 440x440px in search results. Safe zone = center 50% of image.**

**Photo 1 Must-Haves:**
- Framed print **clearly visible** (centered, not cropped)
- High contrast between frame and background
- No text/watermarks that will be cut off
- File size: 2000x2000px minimum (Etsy spec)

**Tools for Mockups:**
- **Free:** Canva (templates for wall mockups, frame mockups)
- **Premium:** Photoshop (advanced shadows, realism)
- **Mid-tier:** Creative Fabrica (high-quality mockup templates)
- **Recommended:** Bulk Mockup, Smartmockups (free tier available)

### Recommended Photo Sequence for Bloom Soft Co.

1. **Primary:** Framed wildflower print on light wall (room mockup) — 1:1 aspect ratio
2. **Secondary:** Flat lay detail shot of print with color/texture
3. **Tertiary:** Gallery wall of 3 complementary wildflower designs
4. **Quaternary:** Phone wallpaper preview (if applicable to product)
5. **Optional:** Size guide chart or lifestyle shot

**Time Investment:** 30–45 min per design (if using templates)

## ETSY ALGORITHM INSIGHTS

### Listing Quality Score Factors

**Etsy's ranking algorithm prioritizes (in order):**

1. **Keyword Relevance (40%):**
   - Title contains exact/variant of search term
   - Tags include search term
   - Description mentions it naturally
   - Tests show listings with keyword in title rank 3–5x higher

2. **Conversion Signals (30%):**
   - Click-through rate (CTR) from search results
   - Add-to-cart rate
   - Completion rate (% of viewers who buy)
   - Higher CTR = algorithm shows more impressions

3. **Review Quality & Recency (15%):**
   - Star rating (4.8+ = boost)
   - Review velocity (recent reviews signal active sales)
   - Review count (100+ reviews = credibility signal)

4. **Listing Freshness (10%):**
   - New listings get 2–3 week "honeymoon period" with extra visibility
   - Refreshed listings (edited, re-photographed) get small bump
   - Monthly renewal/refresh = consistency signal

5. **Shop Authority (5%):**
   - Star Seller status
   - Response rate
   - Complete/cancel rate

### Renewal & Refresh Cadence

**Honeymoon Period (New Listings):**
- Days 1–7: Maximum visibility (test market)
- Days 8–14: Algorithm determining placement based on signals
- Days 15–21: Consolidation (good performers stay, poor performers buried)
- **Action:** Monitor metrics Days 1–7; adjust based on early signals

**Refresh Strategy (Established Listings):**
- Re-photograph/update images every 2–3 months
- Edit title (change word order, test new primary keyword) — triggers algo review
- Add new tags if underperforming
- Refresh description copy (not critical, but minor SEO boost)
- **Frequency:** Once per month minimum for top sellers

**Listing Rotation:**
- Upload 1–2 new designs per week (compounded visibility boost)
- Avoid uploading all 20+ designs at once (algorithm dilutes visibility)
- Space uploads 2–3 days apart for consistent shop activity signal

### Star Seller Requirements (New Rules, July 2025)

**Minimum Thresholds (rolling 3-month window):**
- **5 orders** OR **$300 in sales** (reduced from 10 orders; huge advantage for bundles)
- **4.8+ star rating** (all reviews count equally)
- **95% response rate** to first buyer messages (24-hour window)
- **On-time shipping** (N/A for digital products — automatic pass)

**New Shop Grace Period:**
- First 90 days: Order/sales requirement **waived** if response rate ≥95%
- Means Bloom Soft Co. can achieve Star Seller in Month 1 with just 4.8+ rating + fast messaging

**Benefits of Star Seller:**
- Special badge on shop (builds trust)
- Slight algorithm boost (2–5% visibility increase estimated)
- Messaging priority (appear higher in buyer inbox)
- Auto-renewal eligibility

**Path to Month 1 Star Seller for Bloom:**
- Get 5 sales by Day 30 (realistic with $5–10/day ads)
- Ensure 4.8+ rating (deliver perfect digital files, friendly follow-up)
- Respond to all messages within 12 hours

## ETSY ADS ROI STRATEGY

### Benchmark Targets

**Healthy ROI for Digital Products:** 75–100% (some sellers target 200%+)
- **Translation:** For every $1 spent on ads, return $1.75–$2.00 in revenue

**Example:**
- Ad spend: $100/month
- Expected revenue from ads: $175–$200
- Profit (after Etsy fees): ~$140–$160 (70–80% margin on digital)

### Budget Allocation for Bloom Soft Co.

**Phase 1 (Days 1–14): Testing Budget**
- **Daily budget:** $5–$7
- **Monthly:** $70–$100
- **Goal:** Identify winning products, establish baseline ROAS
- **Targeting:** All products initially; monitor performance daily
- **Action:** Pause underperformers after 3–5 days if ROAS <50%

**Phase 2 (Days 15–30): Scale & Optimize**
- **Daily budget:** $7–$10
- **Monthly:** $100–$150 (if Phase 1 ROI >75%)
- **Goal:** Scale winners, test new product listings
- **Targeting:** Focus 60% of budget on top 3 products; 40% on new listings
- **Action:** Increase daily spend on winners by 20–30% if ROAS >100%

**Phase 3 (Month 2+): Growth Mode**
- **Daily budget:** $10–$20 (if revenue supports it)
- **Sustainability:** Budget should not exceed 20% of projected revenue
- **Goal:** Hit $500–$1,000/month revenue from ads + organic

### Ad Performance Metrics to Track

**Weekly Dashboard (minimum):**
1. **ROAS** (Return on Ad Spend) = Revenue ÷ Ad Spend
2. **CPC** (Cost Per Click) = Ad Spend ÷ Clicks (track by product)
3. **CTR** (Click-Through Rate) = Clicks ÷ Impressions
4. **Conversion Rate** = Purchases ÷ Clicks (target: 3–5% for new shops)
5. **ACoS** (Advertising Cost of Sale) = Ad Spend ÷ Revenue (inverse of ROAS)

**Red Flags (Pause Immediately):**
- ROAS <40% (spending $2.50 to make $1)
- CTR <1% (people not clicking, likely title/image issue)
- Conversion rate <1% (traffic but no buyers, pricing/mockup issue)

### Ad Copy & Image Tips

**Etsy Ads use listing images + auto-written titles.** Optimize:
1. **Image 1 (Thumbnail):** Ensure frame/border is crisp and centered
2. **Title:** Include primary keyword (Etsy auto-uses this)
3. **Tags:** Support secondary keywords (Etsy AI reads these)

### New Etsy Ads Strategy Feature (2025)

Etsy launched strategic ad objectives:
- **Increase Orders** — Higher CPM, volume-focused
- **Balance Orders with Returns** — Moderate spend, steady ROI (recommended)
- **Increase Returns** — Lower cost, efficiency-focused (best for profitability)

**Recommendation for Bloom:** Start with **"Balance Orders with Returns"** (safest for unknown performance data)
